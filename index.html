About NOIR

Who is cooking
your dinner

NOIR opened in 2021 on a quiet street in DHA Phase 8. Chef Faisal Rehman had spent ten years cooking in Lahore and Dubai, and wanted a dinner-only place with a short menu and a busy grill.

The beef comes from a halal farm near Hyderabad. Salmon and scallops arrive twice a week, and the menu changes when the season does.
